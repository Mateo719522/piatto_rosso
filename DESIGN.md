## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, craftsmanship, and convivial spirit of a heritage Italian-Argentine fresh pasta laboratory (*fábrica de pastas caseras*) anchored in Buenos Aires. The aesthetic bridges old-world culinary passion with crisp modern digital commerce, avoiding rustic pastiche in favor of a polished, tactile, and appetizing experience.

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Tactile Warmth & Editorial Elegance:** Luminous warm-cream backgrounds evoke semolina flour, parchment, and marble countertops, avoiding sterile cold whites.
- **Gastronomic Contrast:** Intense pomodoro reds anchor key interactions, balanced by deep charcoal typography and subtle olive/golden accents evoking extra-virgin olive oil and bronze pasta dies.
- **Bespoke Craft Touches:** Distinctive micro-accents like subtle hairline borders, delicate dual-tone striping, and generous rounded contours evoke handmade pasta shapes (ravioli pillows, extruded tagliolini, and folded tortellini).
- **Target Audience:** Connoisseurs of artisanal gastronomy, families ordering Sunday pasta feasts (*los domingos de pastas*), and local epicures seeking authentic, handmade quality with seamless ordering.

## Colors

The palette balances intense, appetizing warmth with deep editorial contrast and utility-focused functional cues.

### Primary Spectrum (Pomodoro Intenso)
- **Primary Base (`#C91818`):** Dominant brand call-to-action, hero badges, and primary interactive surfaces.
- **Primary Deep (`#A30E0E`):** Pressed/hover states, high-emphasis text links, and focused active rings.
- **Primary Tint (`#FDF1F1`):** Warm crimson tint for badge backdrops and gentle contextual alerts.

### Neutral & Foundation (Semolina & Crema Cálida)
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFDF9`):** Pristine culinary parchment for page body.
- **Surface Tint (`#FBF7EE`):** Elevated container cards, navigation bars, and grouped list backgrounds.
- **Surface Subdued (`#F3ECE0`):** Inactive borders, card dividers, and disabled container fills.

### Charcoal & Contrast (Carbón)
- **Charcoal Deep (`#121212`):** Primary display headings, brand marks, and strong emphasis text.
- **Charcoal Body (`#1C1917`):** Standard reading text, descriptive copy, and base iconography.
- **Muted Charcoal (`#5C554E`):** Secondary captions, weight metrics, and cooking instruction subtext.

### Accents & Utility
- **Olive Gold (`#9B8338`):** Quality seals, organic certification badges, and premium batch tags.
- **Direct Order Green (`#25D366`):** Dedicated WhatsApp quick-order interactions, contact floating buttons, and order status indicators.
- **Border Hairline (`#E8E0D2`):** Subtle separator lines framing cards and lists.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast elegance of **Playfair Display** with the geometric precision and open apertures of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Playfair Display:** Reserved exclusively for editorial storytelling, dish titles (e.g., *Sorrentinos de Calabaza y Salvia*, *Tagliatelle al Huevo*), section intros, and signature quotes. It brings romantic warmth and vintage Italian signage flair without sacrificing crispness on high-density screens.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Drives interface usability, pricing tables, nutritional descriptions, packaging weight measures, and action controls. Its balanced proportion guarantees rapid scanning during ordering flows.
- **Editorial Flourishes:** Italicized weights of Playfair Display are leveraged for culinary notes, cooking times (*al dente: 3-4 min*), and family traditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a flexible, content-centric grid designed for visual rhythm, culinary photography showcase, and frictionless purchasing.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Product items default to a single column or 2-column compact grid.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Menus and combos stack in 2 to 3 columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout capped at an inner container max-width of `1280px`, centered with `3.5rem` minimum fluid margins and `2rem` gutters.

### Spacing Principles
- Vertical rhythm follows strict 8px/4px base units.
- Product cards enforce generous inner padding (`space-lg`) to give images breathing room against the warm cream background.
- Dish listings utilize grouped vertical spacing where related ingredients and allergens group at `space-xs`, while sections expand using `space-xl` separators.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold synthetic drop shadows, leaning instead into warm ambient lighting that simulates sunlight striking a clean stone counter.

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Base canvas in `#FFFDF9`. Separated from elevated components using a delicate `1px` border in `#E8E0D2`.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** Soft terracotta-tinted ambient glow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(45, 25, 15, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px rgba(45, 25, 15, 0.02)`, bounded by a subtle `#E8E0D2` stroke.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product):** Lifted state with amplified warmth: `0px 12px 28px rgba(45, 25, 15, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(45, 25, 15, 0.04)`, drawing items forward for selection.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Trays, Modals, & Order Drawer):** Focused elevation: `0px 20px 40px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.14)`.
- **Surface Ribbon Trim:** Selected premium card heads and promotional containers feature an ultra-thin 2px dual pinstripe top border (pomodoro red `#C91818` mirrored by deep charcoal `#1C1917`) to evoke classic Italian grocery packaging.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on roundedness level `2`, mirroring the organic curvature of filled pastas, plates, and ceramic serving dishes while keeping layout structures crisp and responsive.

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** Standard radius of `0.5rem` (`8px`) maintains friendly approachability without becoming childlike.
- **Cards & Visual Containers:** Elevated containers utilize `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to cushion product food imagery.
- **Modals, Floating Panels, & Drawer Tops:** Apply `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) for smooth containment.
- **Interactive Pills:** Category selectors, dietary tags (e.g., *Vegetariano*, *Sin TACC*), and quantity steppers adopt fully rounded pill radii (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Pomodoro Fill):** Solid `#C91818` background, white text, semi-bold `label-lg`, `0.5rem` border-radius, minimum touch target of 48px height. Hover transitions to `#A30E0E` with a subtle `2px` upward lift.
- **Direct Order WhatsApp Button:** Solid `#25D366` background with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text and official icon. Prominently positioned for Argentine user preference regarding instant takeout and family box orders.
- **Secondary (Artisan Outline):** High-contrast `#1C1917` border (1.5px), transparent background, charcoal text. Hover fills with `#F3ECE0`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Plain text in primary `#C91818` with subtle underline animations for menu navigation and footnote actions.

### Cards (Pasta & Product Showcases)
- Rendered on `#FBF7EE` surface with a `1px` border of `#E8E0D2` and Level 1 elevation.
- Product photography uses rounded top corners (`0.875rem`), with a warm inner gradient vignette to ensure light-colored pasta (such as fettuccine) separates clearly from the background.
- Base includes pasta type in `headline-md`, filling description in `body-sm` (`#5C554E`), portion yield (*Rinde 2-3 porciones*), and price tag set in bold sans-serif.

### Quantity Steppers & Add-to-Cart
- Compact pill-shaped modules containing minus, numeric count, and plus icons.
- Border in `#E8E0D2`, fill in `#FFFDF9`. Active tap flashes the brand red tint `#FDF1F1`.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Dietary Tags:** Rounded pill shape with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal padding. Font set in `label-sm`.
- **Artisan Fresh Seal:** Olive-tinted chip (`#9B8338` text on `#F7F4E9` ground) indicating daily production batches (*Elaborado hoy*).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFDF9` with a persistent `1px` border in `#E8E0D2`.
- Focus ring: smooth `2px` outer halo in `#C91818` with an inner border color shift to `#C91818`.
- Placeholder text set in muted warm gray (`#8A8279`).

### Checkboxes & Radios (Sauce & Pairing Pickers)
- Circular radio controls and softly curved square checkboxes (`4px` radius).
- Selected state fills in solid `#C91818` with crisp white checkmark/dot.
- Option rows highlight with a warm cream overlay (`#F3ECE0`) upon hover and selection.

### Custom Component: Domingo Familiar Pack Builder
- A grouped container component featuring step indicators framed by hairline charcoal and crimson ribbons, allowing users to bundle pasta types, artisanal sauces (Tuco, Bolognesa, Pesto Genovés), and grated cheese with instant price recalculation.
